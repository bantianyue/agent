Attention With 4-bit Q (A4Q): a new native NVFP4 attention sm120/121 kernel

tl,dr: Attention With 4-bit Q (A4Q), a new kernel for sm120/sm121 headed to vllm_project as a contribution once some community work lands first.

I thought about holding this blog post back until blocking FlashInfer work lands, but ehh it's fine.

Everyone's been kicking the tires on Claude Fable before our harnesses turn into pumpkins next week, and I'm no exception.

What did I do? I had Claude build a native fp4 attention kernel for sm120/121.

Why did I do it? NVIDIA ships 13,453 pre-compiled attention kernels with TensorRT-LLM, and they're largely compiled for the datacenter parts: sm100a, sm100f, sm103a.

Unfortunately, on a 5090, an RTX PRO 6000, or a DGX Spark there's no directory for your architecture. That's a sensible line for a datacenter-first library to draw; it just means consumer Blackwell needs its own attention path, and that's the gap A4Q fills.

Because of that lack of a kernel from TensorRT-LLM, at time of writing, on sm120/121 the 4-bit KV cache gets unpacked through an fp16 convert chain that burns ~9 instructions per tensor-core op. Prefill attention on the pre-existing fp4 path ran 1.8× slower than plain bf16 attention. In other words, you read a quarter of the bytes and it takes nearly twice as long.

I knew roughly why, but "roughly" isn't a number, so this campaign was about figuring that out.

In more detail. Our new kernel, A4Q, runs QKᵀ on consumer Blackwell's block-scaled fp4 MMA instead: straight from the cache, zero dequant, and the whole Gemma family comes along (well, with my patches).

I proved it out on consumer Blackwell (sm120) end-to-end; the Spark (sm121) is bit-identical at the ISA level and now measured serving in its own right on a GB10 — passkey retrieval green and the long-context decode-tok/s curve below both ran on Spark silicon, A4Q provably firing.

Bottom line:

The measured win — on wide-KV models. Gemma-4-26B-A4B decodes 2.01× faster end to end at 100k context, Gemma-4-31B 1.44× — NVFP4 KV on both sides, batch-1 steady state, on a DGX Spark. That's the headline, and it's the honest one: a real long-context serving speedup on hardware you can actually buy.

The kernel is faster still; the model decides how much you feel. The QKᵀ kernel alone runs 8.9× over the shipping fp4 path (12× at 100k) and beats bf16 — but that's measured at wide KV (16 heads). End to end you get it only where attention is a real slice of the step and the heads are wide. Narrow the KV and it evaporates.

The KV-width law — the one thing to remember. Across six models the end-to-end win sorts perfectly by KV width (num_kv_heads × head_dim): everything ≥4096 wins (both Gemma-4s), everything ≤1024 washes — Nemotron (Mamba hybrid), Qwen3.5/3.6 (linear hybrid), and even pure-softmax Qwen3-30B (narrow GQA). It isn't softmax-vs-hybrid and it isn't head_dim alone; it's KV width. The field traded that away for GQA around 2024; Gemma-4 kept it, and A4Q rewards exactly that choice.

Correctness and memory are universal — only the speedup is Gemma's. Six models (Gemma-4, Nemotron-3, two Qwens) serve with NVFP4 weights and NVFP4 KV at passkey 12/12 and GSM8K parity, and every one gets the 4× KV-memory saving (more context fits). A4Q serves them all correctly — it just accelerates the wide-KV ones. (It's also the only path that runs Gemma-4's 512-wide VO-split heads on the tensor core at all; every other engine punts them to software.)

Bit-exact, with an asterisk worth stating plainly. The arithmetic is exact: the QK path matches a dequantized reference at max_abs_diff = 0.0 (fp4 × fp8-scale into an fp32 accumulator has nothing left to round). The approximation is quantizing Q to four bits — and its cost at the model level is nothing you can measure (the passkey/GSM8K rows below).

Why would you want this? If your model's decode step is bottlenecked on attention, which is exactly where Gemma lives.

Where might you not want this? If your model isn't attention-bound. A Mamba-hybrid like Nemotron-3-Nano spends most of its decode step outside attention, so A4Q is an honest wash there.
