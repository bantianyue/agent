\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:concl}

\noindent
This paper shows that RL rollout can be efficient 
without compromising the algorithmic equivalence of the original training with 
fast speculative decoding. 
{\sys} makes speculative rollout efficient with two novel techniques: 
(1) a \emph{Decoupled speculation} method that improves the speculation efficiency 
for common training setups, 
and (2) a \emph{Fastest-of-N speculation} method that 
dynamically adapts the draft method
% approximates the optimal draft method 
during the rollout process.
{\sys} improves the training efficiency by {1.4--2.3}\,$\times$ compared to the state-of-the-art baselines,
and is {1.1--2.6}\,$\times$ faster than vanilla speculative rollout.
